# 🚀 Agentic AI Workshop – One Person Company

**Aditya University | Department of Technical Training (DoTT)**

## 📌 About

A **12-day hands-on Agentic AI Workshop** focused on learning, building, and integrating intelligent AI agents to automate real-world business operations.

The goal is to develop a **One Person Company**, where multiple AI agents collaborate to handle customer support, orders, records, and invoices with human supervision.

This repository contains my daily learning, practical implementations, and workshop projects.
